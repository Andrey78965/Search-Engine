airplanes have transformed the world by making long distance travel fast and possible the first successful flight was made by the wright brothers in nineteen three since then aviation has advanced dramatically modern aircraft are marvels of engineering with powerful engines sophisticated control systems and lightweight materials jet engines allow planes to fly at high altitudes and speeds commercial aviation connects cities and countries around the world cargo planes transport goods across oceans military aircraft protect nations and support global operations the design of an airplane involves careful study of aerodynamics lift drag and stability pilots rely on advanced instruments and computer systems to navigate safely air traffic control manages thousands of flights every day to prevent accidents the future of aviation includes electric planes supersonic travel and autonomous aircraft that can fly without pilots airplanes continue to push the boundaries of technology and human achievement
