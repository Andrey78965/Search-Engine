robots are machines designed to perform tasks automatically they can be found in factories homes hospitals and even space the first industrial robots were introduced in the middle of the twentieth century and they quickly changed manufacturing by making production faster and more precise modern robots use sensors cameras and artificial intelligence to interact with their environment some robots are built to handle dangerous jobs like exploring deep oceans or working in nuclear facilities others assist doctors during surgery or help people with disabilities at home educational robots teach children programming and engineering skills the design of a robot involves mechanical engineering electronics and software systems that control movement and decision making as technology improves robots become more capable and more integrated into daily life the future may bring robots that work alongside humans in offices schools and public spaces automation will continue to grow and robots will play a central role in shaping the future of work and society
